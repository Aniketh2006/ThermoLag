# 3-minute ThermoLag SIH demo

1. **Open dashboard** — introduce ThermoLag as a ward-level heat-health early-warning system.
2. **Point to the KPI row** — show ward coverage, high/extreme wards, peak risk and the approval queue.
3. **Click Old Delhi** — show the red ward, UTCI/WBGT, confidence and the plain-language “why this ward?” explanation.
4. **Move across the 5-day forecast** — explain that the system is designed for a 3–5 day operational lead time, not only a current-temperature warning.
5. **Switch to Humid heat** — show that humidity changes the thermal-stress/risk profile.
6. **Switch to Mild baseline** — show the system can return to low-risk conditions.
7. **Create approval-gated alert** — explain that recommendations are prepared first and require city approval.
8. **Approve** — show the simulated CAP release and mention that real SMS/WhatsApp/NDMA Sachet integration is a later operational step.
9. **If asked about mortality** — say the demo intentionally shows a Heat-Health Risk Proxy. The SIH proposal upgrades this to a calibrated Mortality Risk Index only after a city pilot provides de-identified ward-day health outcomes.
